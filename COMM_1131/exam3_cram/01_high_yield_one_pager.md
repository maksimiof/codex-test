## 2) 12 Fast Facts (High Value)

1. **PrEP** can reduce HIV risk by up to **99%** when taken correctly.  
2. **PEP** must start within **72 hours**.  
3. **Plan B (LNG)**: best ≤72h, usable to 120h, OTC.  
4. **Ella (UPA)**: works through 120h, Rx needed.  
5. **LARC** (IUD/implant): <1% failure typical use.  
6. **Combined OCPs** suppress GnRH → ↓FSH/LH → prevent ovulation.  
7. **Contempt** is Gottman’s most destructive horseman.  
8. **Collaborating** = high assertive + high cooperative.  
9. **Phubbing** = phone snubbing; harms relationship quality.  
10. **Spectatoring** = monitoring appearance during sex, reducing enjoyment.  
11. **Trauma-informed care** starts with safety + choice + trust.  
12. **Older adults still need STI counseling** (don’t miss this stereotype question).

## 3) Confusable Pairs (Exam Favorite)

| Pair | Correct Distinction |
|---|---|
| **PrEP vs PEP** | PrEP = before exposure (prevention); PEP = after exposure (emergency, ≤72h) |
| **Cultivation vs Social Learning** | Cultivation = worldview shaping; Social Learning = modeling behavior |
| **Open relationship vs polyamory** | Open = often primary couple + outside sexual partners; polyamory = multiple consensual romantic loves |
| **Forgiveness vs reconciliation** | Forgiveness can be one-sided internal process; reconciliation requires mutual repair |
| **Arranged vs forced marriage** | Arranged can be consensual; forced is non-consensual |

## 4) Memory Hooks (30-second recall)

- **FRIES**: “Consent is like fries — can be refused anytime.”  
- **Horsemen antidotes**: Criticism→gentle start-up; Contempt→appreciation; Defensiveness→take responsibility; Stonewalling→self-soothe.  
- **Scripts stack**: **C-I-I** = Cultural, Interpersonal, Intrapsychic.  
- **TKI map**: Collaborate = “both matter most.”

---

## 5) Last 10-Minute Strategy

1. **Read stem first** for what is being tested (definition vs scenario).  
2. If two answers look right, choose the one with **communication process** language, not moral judgment language.  
3. On stigma/disparity items, prefer **structural/intersectional** explanation over individual-blame framing.  
4. On consent items, choose active/ongoing/reversible wording.  
5. On pharmacist scenario items, choose **nonjudgmental + accurate + options + referral when needed**.
