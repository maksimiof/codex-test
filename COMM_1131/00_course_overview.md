### Course Description
COMM 1131 examines how communication shapes sexual behavior, intimate relationships, and personal identity. It draws on communication theory, sociology, psychology, and public health to help students understand and navigate complex interpersonal dynamics.

---

### Major Topic Areas

| # | Topic | Key Questions |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | **Communication Foundations** | How do we send, receive, and interpret messages in relationships? |
| 2 | **Gender & Sexuality** | How are gender identity and sexual orientation constructed and communicated? |
| 3 | **Consent & Ethics** | What is affirmative consent? How is it negotiated and communicated? |
| 4 | **Relationship Types & Structures** | What are the varieties of intimate relationships, and how do they function? |
| 5 | **Sexual Health** | How do STIs, contraception, and reproductive health intersect with communication? |
| 6 | **Conflict & Communication Styles** | How do we navigate disagreement, power, and negotiation in relationships? |
| 7 | **Media & Sexuality** | How does media shape norms, expectations, and behavior? |
| 8 | **Diverse Identities & Inclusion** | How do intersectionality and cultural context influence relationship communication? |
